Overview

RhinoRoTex is the vortex flow control chamber in the RHINO range, sold as ROTEX600 to ROTEX1200. A passive vortex regulator on the outlet holds stormwater discharge to a pre-set maximum rate, protecting downstream drainage from overload during heavy rainfall.

The unit fits curved or flat surfaces as standard. It is supplied factory-fitted in an HDPE twinwall chamber, arriving on site ready for connection and backfill with no benching, or as a standalone unit for fitting into an existing concrete chamber.

How the vortex works

Swirling water does the regulating, so there are no moving parts. Stormwater entering the flow controller is set rotating; as inflow rises the vortex strengthens and centrifugal forces restrict the flow, holding the outflow to a pre-set maximum rate even as storm conditions vary. The excess is held back in the chamber and upstream storage and released as the storm passes.

How it works - vortex, sump, emergency bypass
1

Vortex control

The flow controller is mounted on the outlet. Water entering it is set rotating, and the vortex strengthens as the inflow rises, so centrifugal forces restrict the flow and the discharge stays at or below the pre-set rate. There is no external power and nothing mechanical to fail.

2

Storage and sump

Inflow above the controlled rate backs up in the chamber and the upstream attenuation, then drains down as the storm passes. The sump below the outlet collects silt and debris so they can be removed during routine maintenance.

3

Emergency bypass

An emergency bypass door on the flow controller is opened by a cable from the surface. The chamber can be drained for inspection, or a blockage cleared, without anyone entering it.

4

Factory-fitted or standalone

The factory-fitted option arrives as a complete system ready for connection and backfill, removing the cost and hazard of in-ground benching. The standalone unit mounts on the curved or flat wall of an existing concrete chamber.

Installation

  • Bed and surround to suit the traffic loading and ground conditions
  • Allow for variable groundwater levels and flotation
  • Set the outlet to the design invert; the flow controller is factory-fitted
  • Keep the bypass cable free and reachable from cover level
  • Have the installation signed off by a suitably qualified professional
