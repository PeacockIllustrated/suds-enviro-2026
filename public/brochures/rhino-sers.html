Overview

The RHINO SERS Series is engineered for the efficient catchment and removal of silt and debris from impermeable or hard surface areas such as paths, driveways, patios, roofs, car parks, roads and highways. A primary and secondary filtration system, with a removable silt bucket, gives effective primary treatment of surface runoff, improves downstream water quality and reduces the risk of flooding.

Each chamber is manufactured from high-density polyethylene using extrusion and thermoforming, and delivered fully assembled as a one-piece unit ready for installation, for light-duty to full-traffic loading applications.

How the silt bucket works

Surface water enters through the inlet and larger debris is intercepted as it drops into the chamber. The removable filter basket, or silt bucket, sits in the base below the pipe inverts and captures finer sediment before water leaves through the outlet. Maintenance is a lift-out and empty operation.

How it works - filtration, silt bucket, maintenance
1

Primary filtration

Runoff from paths, roofs, car parks and highways enters through the inlet. Larger silt and debris are intercepted in the chamber rather than carried on into the downstream pipework.

2

Secondary filtration

The removable silt bucket sits in the base below the inlet and outlet inverts and captures finer sediment. The dual system improves downstream water quality and reduces the risk of blockages and flooding.

3

Simple maintenance

The chamber is designed for above-ground inspection and maintenance. The silt bucket is lifted out, emptied and returned, keeping downstream drainage and attenuation clear.

4

Robust one-piece build

A thermoformed base gives structural strength and anti-fracture properties. Optimised internal surfaces help water flow and reduce blockages, and HDPE resists hydrogen sulphide, corrosive acids and bases.
