Overview

The RHINO grease separator is a below-ground unit for larger-scale fats, oils and grease (FOG) management. It is designed for high-output commercial kitchens, food processing facilities and catering operations that need compliant pre-treatment before discharge to sewer.

Each unit is sized to the site's peak covers per day and design flow rate. The multi-chamber design gives enhanced FOG separation, and the large grease storage capacity reduces how often the unit has to be emptied.

How it works

Kitchen waste water flows through the chambers in turn. Fats, oils and grease rise and are held back in storage while the separated water passes forward to the outlet. An integral sample point allows the discharge to be sampled for trade effluent monitoring.

Grease trap or separator

Smaller kitchens can use the RHINO GT grease trap range, rated from 150 to 600 covers per day and fitted under the sink or on the floor. The below-ground grease separator is for higher-output sites that need more storage and a sample point for monitoring.
