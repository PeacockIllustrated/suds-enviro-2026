Overview

RHINO septic tanks are rotationally moulded HDPE tanks for primary and secondary wastewater treatment at properties without a mains sewer connection. They are designed for domestic and small commercial applications, discharging to a soakaway, drainage field or watercourse.

One-piece construction eliminates leakage risk, and the tanks are light enough to install without heavy plant. Integral dip pipes and internal baffles are moulded in, and the tanks suit variable ground conditions.

Treatment levels

Primary: a gravity-based system with the septic tank connected to a drainage field.

Secondary: the septic tank with a sand filter or packaged treatment plant. Secondary treatment is required for discharge to a watercourse.

Design compliance

  • BS EN 12566-1:2016 prefabricated septic tanks
  • BS 6297:2007+A1:2008 drainage fields
  • Environment Agency General Binding Rules
  • Building Regulations Approved Document H2

Installation and maintenance

The tanks are light enough to be placed without heavy plant and suit variable ground conditions. Drainage fields are designed and installed to BS 6297. Desludging is recommended annually, subject to use.
