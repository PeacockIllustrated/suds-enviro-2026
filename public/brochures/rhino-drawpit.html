Overview

RhinoDuct is a range of rectangular drawpit chambers for ducted utility, telecoms and cable networks. Each chamber is built from interlocking 150 mm deep sections, stacked on site to reach the depth required.

Footprints run from 450 x 450 mm in the Standard range to 1500 x 1500 mm in the Plus and Plus + Reinforcing ranges.

How it builds

Choose the footprint (dimensions A x B), then stack sections until the chamber reaches finished level. Every section is 150 mm deep (dimension C), so the depth is set on site without cutting.

Loading and compliance

  • Tested in accordance with EN 124:1994, classified E600 (all three ranges)
  • NJUG Volume 4 guidelines
  • Design Manual for Roads and Bridges (DMRB), published by National Highways

Features & benefits

  • Interlocking 150 mm sections, depth set on site
  • 19 footprints from 450 x 450 to 1500 x 1500 mm
  • Reinforcing option across the Plus sizes
  • Tested to EN 124, classified E600
  • Manufactured from recycled polymer
  • Lighter than concrete to handle and install

Ranges

  • Standard (SERD): 450 x 450 to 750 x 750 mm
  • Plus (SERDP): 900 x 450 to 1500 x 1500 mm
  • Plus + Reinforcing (SERDPR): the Plus footprints with reinforcing

Applications

Telecoms and fibre ducting, electrical and utility cable access, and service chambers in footways and highways.

Specifying

  • Footprint A x B from the size range
  • Finished depth, as a number of 150 mm sections
  • Standard, Plus or Plus + Reinforcing
  • Load class for the location
  • Cover type: solid or grated
  • Duct entry positions and sizes

Reading the codes

The code gives the range, then A and B in centimetres, then the 150 mm section depth. SERD6045150 is a Standard chamber 600 x 450 mm built from 150 mm sections. Plus codes start SERDP and Plus + Reinforcing codes start SERDPR.
