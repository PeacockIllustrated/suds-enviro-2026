Overview

RhinoPod is a floating cartridge filter that intercepts dissolved-phase pollutants in surface water runoff, including zinc, copper, phosphate (PO4) and polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs).

Runoff from car parks, roads and industrial yards carries sediment, plastics and oils alongside invisible contaminants such as dissolved metals, phosphates and hydrocarbons. RhinoPod targets the dissolved fraction that conventional separators leave behind.

How it works

The pod needs no mounting. It is gravity-held in standing water, and self-guided flow channels carry water through the filter cartridge. There is no electrical demand and head loss is low, so pods can be deployed at local drainage points across a site.

Two-stage treatment

Paired with the SudSceptor hydrodynamic separator, which captures suspended solids, grit, oils and hydrocarbons, RhinoPod forms a dual-stage treatment train covering both gross and dissolved pollutants.

Features & benefits

  • Targets dissolved zinc, copper, phosphate and PAHs
  • No mounting, gravity-held in standing water
  • Self-guided flow channels
  • No electrical demand, low head loss
  • Retrofit-ready, no disruption to surface finishes
  • Surface or subsurface installation

Where it goes

  • Road gullies (450 to 600 mm)
  • GRP or precast chambers, with 1 to 4 pods
  • Oil interceptors and retrofit drainage units
  • The SERPOD1850 filter tank, with six pods

Applications

Petrol stations, logistics yards, commercial estates and depots: any site with several drainage points where pollutant interception is needed at each outfall.

Specifying, compliance and contact

Deployment options

  • Standalone filter in a road gully, GRP or precast chamber, or an existing oil interceptor
  • Multi-pod chamber with 1 to 4 pods in a GRP or precast chamber
  • Filter tank such as the SERPOD1850, with six pods and a coalescing filter
  • Treatment train with the SudSceptor installed in line upstream of attenuation or discharge points

The SudSceptor pairing

The SudSceptor is a vortex-based separator that captures suspended solids, grit, oils and hydrocarbons. RhinoPod then intercepts the dissolved-phase pollutants. The modular pairing supports surface and subsurface installation, designed in from the outset or added by retrofit.

Tell us about the site

  • Number and type of drainage points
  • Gully or chamber sizes, new or existing
  • Catchment area and use (yard, forecourt, car park)
  • Pollutants of concern and any discharge consent
  • Whether a SudSceptor or separator is planned upstream

We provide layout support and chamber specifications for RhinoPod schemes.

Compliance

  • Tested against pollutants relevant to the EU Water Framework Directive (2000/60/EC)
  • Supports Schedule 3 SuDS design guidance

Why decentralise

For sites with multiple drainage points, intercepting pollutants at source gives greater control and often better cost-efficiency than one large central system. Pods placed in local gullies, separators or chamber-based filtration points shorten pipe runs, lower capital cost and build compliance in at every vulnerable outfall.

Performance at a glance

  • RhinoPod alone: high removal of zinc, copper and phosphate
  • Medium removal of PAHs and hydrocarbons
  • Low removal of suspended solids, so pair with a separator upstream

One tank, two stages

In the SERPOD1850 filter tank, flow from the Ø300 inlet passes through a bay of six RhinoPods and then a coalescing filter before leaving by the Ø300 outlet. Access shafts sit over the pod bay and the filter. See page 3 for the general arrangement.

Maintenance

Pods need no mounting or fixings. Ask our technical team for inspection and replacement intervals to suit the site loading.
